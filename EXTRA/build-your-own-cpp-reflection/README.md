## What is Reflection?

What is Reflection?\
Chinese: 反射\
It can be hard to understand by literal understanding.\
Imagine you are a running program in the computer. You have no idea what do you look like, but somehow you got a mirror. And from the `reflection`, you can observe yourself.\
The reflection is that (not absolutely and not precisely): you can get the runtime information of the program, including what the type of variables is, what function do it have.
